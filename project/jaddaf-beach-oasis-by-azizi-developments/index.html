Q4 2028

Two 16-storey towers in Al Jaddaf, close to Dubai Creek and Healthcare City, with studios, one- and two-bedroom apartments and penthouses, about half built at the last update. Facilities include adults' and kids' pools, separate gyms, a cinema hall, an indoor kids' play area and ground-floor retail and dining. The plan is 10% on booking, 40% during construction and 50% at handover in Q4 2028.
