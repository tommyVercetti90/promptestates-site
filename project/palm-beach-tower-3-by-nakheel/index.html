A 49-storey tower in the Dubai Marina area that was about three-quarters built at the last update, with apartments, penthouses and villas. Features include a spa, a rooftop restaurant with a viewing gallery, an infinity pool and a viewing deck. The plan is 20% on booking, 40% during construction and 40% at handover in Q4 2026.
