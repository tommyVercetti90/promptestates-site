Starline Beach Residences is a residential development by Sobha Realty in Siniya Island, Umm Al Quwain, offering homes from 1 bedroom to 3 bedrooms. The building plan is 2G + 7 floors, 2G + 9 floors. Handover is planned for Q2 2029, with construction at 0% by the last inspection. Units on sale start from AED 455,287. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 40% during construction, 40% on handover. Residents get natural beach, adventure water sports, infinity swimming pool, community centre. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
