In Business Bay, Binghatti Developers is building Burj Binghatti Jacob and Co Residences: homes from 2 bedrooms to 4 bedrooms. It is a branded residence. The building plan is 6B + G + 5P + 98 floors + R. Handover is planned for Q2 2027, with construction at 52.0% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 8.40M. Payment plan: 50% on booking, 20% during construction, 30% on handover. Residents get lobby lounge, gymnasium, concierge service, chauffeur service. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
