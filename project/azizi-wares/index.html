Q1 2028

A compact seven-storey building in the Jebel Ali Freezone extension with studios, one- and two-bedroom apartments over shops, at one of the lowest entry prices in Azizi's range. Residents get adults' and kids' pools, a clubhouse, gym, play area, multipurpose hall and a private cinema. Buyers pay 10% on booking, 40% during construction and 50% at handover in Q1 2028.
