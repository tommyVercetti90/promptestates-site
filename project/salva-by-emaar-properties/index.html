Salva is an Emaar Properties project in The Heights with homes from 3 bedrooms to 4 bedrooms. The building plan is G + 1floor, G + 2 floors. Handover is planned for Q3 2030, with construction at 0.5% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 6.76M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 70% during construction, 20% on handover. Residents get wellness centre, clubhouse, sport courts, community pool. Furnishing: only kitchen.
