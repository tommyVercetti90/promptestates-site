Inaura Hotels & Residences Downtown is a 42-floor branded tower by Arada on the Downtown Dubai and Business Bay edge. It offers one- and two-bedroom apartments, four-bedroom homes and a five-bedroom duplex, semi-furnished, with a sky lounge, indoor and infinity pools, spa, gym, yoga and pilates studio, cinema and music room. Sales started in late 2025; handover is planned for Q2 2030 on a 10/40/50 plan.
