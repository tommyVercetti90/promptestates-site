Damac Islands Phase 2 is an DAMAC Properties project in Damac Suncity with homes from 4 bedrooms to 5 bedrooms. The building plan is G + 1floor, G + 1floor + R. Handover is planned for Q2 2030, with construction at 0.3% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 3.33M. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 55% during construction, 25% on handover. Residents get botanic garden, whispering waterfall, solar charging stations, wellness area. Furnishing: only kitchen.
