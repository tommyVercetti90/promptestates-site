Le Château is an Beyond project in Al Hamra, Ras Al Khaimah with homes from 1 bedroom to 3 bedrooms. The building plan is 3B + G + 31 floors. Handover is planned for Q4 2029. Units on sale start from AED 2.15M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 40% during construction, 50% on handover. Residents get infinity pool, outdoor dining & barbecue area, landscaped gardens, event room & library. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
