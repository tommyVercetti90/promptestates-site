Eleve is a residential development by Deyaar in Jebel Ali Freezone Extension, offering homes from 2 bedrooms to 3 bedrooms. The building plan is 2B + G + 4P + 33 floors. Handover is planned for Q2 2027, with construction at 20.0% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.60M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 20% during construction, 40% on handover, 30% after handover. Residents get restaurant, swimming pool, cafè, outdoor play area. Furnishing: only kitchen.
