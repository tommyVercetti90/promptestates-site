Elo 2 and 3 Damac is a residential development by DAMAC Properties in Damac Hills 2, offering 2 bedrooms homes. The building plan is G + P + 14 floors. Handover is planned for Q3 2027, with construction at 6.4% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.35M. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 30% during construction, 50% on handover.
