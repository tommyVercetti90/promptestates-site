In Dubai Maritime City, DAMAC Properties is building Chelsea Residences: homes from 1 bedroom to 2 bedrooms. It is a branded residence. The building plan is G + 2P + 25 floors, G + 2P + 28 floors, G + 2P + 30 floors, G + 2P + 33 floors, G + 2P + 30 floors, G + 2P + 33 floors. Handover is planned for Q4 2029, with construction at 6.5% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 2.46M. Payment plan: 5% on booking, 55% during construction, 40% on handover. Residents get treatment spa, starlite wellness center, forest relaxation pods, rain therapy. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
