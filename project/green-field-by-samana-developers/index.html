A 14-storey building in the Al Warsan area near International City with one- to 2.5-bedroom apartments, offices and shops. Facilities include a pool and kids' pool, jacuzzi, splash area, outdoor cinema and outdoor gym. Buyers pay 10% on booking, 10% during construction and 80% at handover in Q1 2029.
