River Cove Residences at Sobha City is a residential development by Sobha Realty in Abu Dhabi, offering homes from 3 bedrooms to 4 bedrooms. The building plan is 11 floors. Handover is planned for Q4 2029, with construction at 0% by the last inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.23M. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 40% during construction, 40% on handover. Residents get swimming pool, kids play area, golf areas, meditation zones. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
