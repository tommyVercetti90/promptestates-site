The Symphony by Imtiaz

Q2 2029
Commercial units from AED 2.45M

Installment plan35% before handover, 65% after
OverviewPhotosUnitsPaymentAnalyticsLocation

A 34-storey branded tower in Bukadra, near Ras Al Khor and Dubai Creek, with one- to four-bedroom apartments and duplexes plus offices and shops. Amenities include an observatory deck, clubhouse, library, pool, gym, steam room and sauna. Buyers pay 20% on booking, 15% during construction and 65% at handover in Q2 2029.
