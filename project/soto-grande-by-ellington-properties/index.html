Soto Grande is a residential development by Ellington Properties in Al Hamra, Ras Al Khaimah, offering homes from 1 bedroom to 3 bedrooms. The building plan is G + 22 floors, G + 23 floors. Handover is planned for Q4 2029. Units on sale start from AED 1.85M. Residents get access to public beach, kids play area, courtyard access, fitness studio. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
