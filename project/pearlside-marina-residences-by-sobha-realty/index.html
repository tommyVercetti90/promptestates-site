Pearlside Marina Residences is a Sobha Realty project in Siniya Island, Umm Al Quwain with homes from 1 bedroom to 3 bedrooms. The building plan is G + P + 8 floors. Handover is planned for Q1 2030, with construction at 0% by the last inspection. Units on sale start from AED 382,452. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 40% during construction, 40% on handover. Residents get golf course, waterfront marina, seaside promenade, infinity pools. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
