Binghatti Starlight is a residential development by Binghatti Developers in Al Jaddaf, offering homes from studio to 2 bedrooms. The building plan is 3B + G + 3P + 20 floors + R. Handover is planned for Q4 2026, with construction at 63.1% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 928,000. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 50% during construction, 30% on handover. Residents get state-of-the-art fitness centre, swimming pool, landscaped outdoor spaces. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
