Albero is an Emaar Properties project in Dubai Creek Harbour with homes from 1 bedroom to 3 bedrooms. The building plan is B + G + 3P + 53 floors; G + 1floor + R. Handover is planned for Q3 2029, with construction at 11.0% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.81M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 70% during construction, 20% on handover. Residents get multipurpose zone, kids play area, gym & outdoor fitness, community garden. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
