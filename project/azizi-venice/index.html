Azizi Venice

Q4 2026
from AED 710K
DeveloperAzizi Developments
LocationDubai South
Installment plan25% before handover, 75% after
OverviewPhotosUnitsPaymentAnalyticsLocation

A mixed-use apartment cluster inside Azizi Venice, a master community in Dubai South built around a swimmable lagoon, with buildings of 10 to 13 floors over retail. The community plan includes a Friday mosque, an opera house and museum, a waterpark, spa, restaurants and jogging and cycling tracks. About three-quarters built at the last update; 25% is paid during construction and 75% at handover in Q4 2026.
