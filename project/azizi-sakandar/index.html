Azizi Sakandar

Q4 2027
from AED 1.05M
DeveloperAzizi Developments

Installment plan50% before handover, 50% after

An 11-storey building in Jebel Ali Village, mostly studios with some one- and two-bedroom apartments. It has a gym, indoor and outdoor kids' areas, a cinema, multipurpose hall and pool. The plan is 20% on booking, 30% during construction and 50% at handover in Q4 2027.
