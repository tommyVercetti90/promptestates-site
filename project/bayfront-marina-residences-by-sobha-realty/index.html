Bayfront Marina Residences is a residential development by Sobha Realty in Siniya Island, Umm Al Quwain, offering homes from 1 bedroom to 3 bedrooms. The building plan is 3 Towers: G + 9 floors. Handover is planned for Q3 2029, with construction at 0% by the last inspection. Units on sale start from AED 370,567. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 40% during construction, 40% on handover. Residents get access to the golf course, yacht club, café & restaurants, brimming promenade. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
