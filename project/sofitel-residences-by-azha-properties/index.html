Sofitel Residences is a low-rise branded building (G+2P+11) by Azha Properties in Downtown Dubai, run with the Sofitel hotel brand. It offers two- and three-bedroom apartments and a four-bedroom penthouse, delivered semi-furnished, with a rooftop pool and bar, Sofitel health club, gym, cinema room and a kids' play area with a library. Handover is planned for Q2 2027; the plan is 10% on booking, 30% in three payments during construction and 60% on handover.
