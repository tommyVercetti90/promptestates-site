In Zabeel, Arada is building Akala Residences: homes from 1 bedroom to 6 bedrooms. The building plan is 4B + G + 3P + 48 floors + R. Handover is planned for Q4 2029, with construction at 1.9% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 3.80M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 30% during construction, 60% on handover. Residents get infinity pool, yoga space, spa, cinema. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
