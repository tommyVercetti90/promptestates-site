Damac Riverside Views is an DAMAC Properties project in Damac Riverside with homes from 1 bedroom to 2 bedrooms. The building plan is G + 5 floors, G + 6 floors, G + 8 floors. Handover is planned for Q4 2028, with construction at 8.3% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.36M. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 30% during construction, 50% on handover. Residents get island restaurant, hydroponic farm, callisthenics stations, essential oil lakes.
