In Meydan, Binghatti Developers is building Maybach 6: homes from studio to 3 bedrooms. It is a branded residence. The building plan is B + G + M + 5P + 30 floors + 2M + R, B + G + M + 5P + 34 floors + 2M + R. Handover is planned for Q2 2028, with construction at 17.5% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.35M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 20% during construction, 70% on handover. Residents get swimming pool, garden area, multipurpose sport court, outdoor yoga area. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
