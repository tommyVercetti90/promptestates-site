Elitz 2 by Danube Properties

Elitz 2 is an off-plan project by Danube Properties in Jumeirah Village Circle, with studios and 1-bedroom to 3-bedroom apartments. Handover is planned for Q4 2027. The payment plan is 30% during construction, 70% on handover. It has 801 homes in total. Shared facilities include aquatic gym, bbq area, beauty parlour, business center and cabana seating.
