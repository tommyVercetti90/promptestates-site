Arancia Yards 2 is a residential development by Beyond in City Of Arabia, offering homes from 1 bedroom to 3 bedrooms. The building plan is Buildings A-C: G + 7 floors, Building D: G + 5 floors. Handover is planned for Q2 2029, with construction at 0% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.52M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 30% during construction, 60% on handover. Residents get gym, yoga area, swimming pool, kids' play areas. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
