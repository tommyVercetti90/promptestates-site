OverviewPhotosUnitsPaymentAnalyticsLocation

In Business Bay, Binghatti Developers is building Binghatti Skyrise: homes from studio to 4 bedrooms. The building plan is 3 Towers: G + 4P + 47 floors. Handover is planned for Q4 2027, with construction at 35.9% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.20M. Payment plan: 50% on booking, 20% during construction, 30% on handover. Residents get private pool with jacuzzi, badminton court, landscaped gardens, sunken seating. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
