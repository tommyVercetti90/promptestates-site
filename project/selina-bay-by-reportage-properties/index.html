In Yas Island, Reportage Properties is building Selina Bay: homes from studio to 4 bedrooms. The building plan is 2B + G + 18 floors. Handover is planned for Q4 2027, with construction at 10.5% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 1.25M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 10% during construction, 80% on handover. Residents get 2 kids play area, 2 large swimming pool, 2 kids swimming pool, 2 gymnasium. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
