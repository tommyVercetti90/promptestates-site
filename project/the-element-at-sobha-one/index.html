The Element at Sobha One is a residential development by Sobha Realty in Ras Al Khor, offering homes from 1 bedroom to 4 bedrooms. The building plan is B + G + 67 floors + R. Handover is planned for Q4 2028, with construction at 17.0% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 498,624. Payment plan: 20% on booking, 40% during construction, 40% on handover. Residents get swimming pool, jacuzzi, lawn with seating, pet-friendly spaces. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
