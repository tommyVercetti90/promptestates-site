Sorrento is an DAMAC Properties project in Damac Lagoons with homes from studio to 1 bedroom. The building plan is Towers: G + 7 floors. Handover is planned for Q1 2030, with construction at 0.3% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 852,000. Payment plan: 5% on booking, 45% during construction, 50% on handover. Residents get community amphitheatre, coffee shop, culinary zone, entertainment hub. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
