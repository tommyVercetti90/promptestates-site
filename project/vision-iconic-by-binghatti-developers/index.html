Vision Iconic is a residential development by Binghatti Developers in Meydan, offering homes from 1 bedroom to 5 bedrooms. It is a branded residence. The building plan is B + G + 5P + 65 floors + 9M + R. Handover is planned for Q4 2028, with construction at 0% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 2.25M. Payment plan: 50% on booking, 20% during construction, 30% on handover. Residents get swimming pool, garden area, luxury retail, multipurpose sport court. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
