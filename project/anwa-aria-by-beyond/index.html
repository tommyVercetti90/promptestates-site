Anwa Aria is a residential development by Beyond in Maritime City, offering homes from 1 bedroom to 4 bedrooms. The building plan is G + 37 floors. Handover is planned for Q1 2027, with construction at 73.54% by the last DLD inspection. Units on sale start from AED 2.82M. Payment plan: 10% on booking, 40% during construction, 30% on handover, 20% after handover. Residents get well-equipped gymnasium, yoga studio, outdoor terraces, kids’ play area. Homes are delivered semi furnished.
