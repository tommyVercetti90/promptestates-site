Business Bay: off-plan property and the area

Business Bay is a central Dubai district along the Dubai Canal, next to Downtown, Burj Khalifa and Dubai Mall.

In short
  • A mix of office and residential towers.
  • Next to Downtown Dubai and Burj Khalifa.
  • A promenade along the Dubai Canal.
  • Has a metro station.

What the area is like

It is one of the city’s main business quarters, and many residential developments have appeared here in recent years. Most parts of Dubai are quick to reach from it.

Who it suits

People who work in the centre and want to live nearby, and investors who value a central location and tenant demand.

Transport

The Business Bay metro station is nearby, along with Sheikh Zayed Road and Al Khail Road. Downtown Dubai is a walk away along the canal promenade.

What homes are sold here

Apartments in high-rise residential towers, including ones with canal and Burj Khalifa views. There are many new projects.

Frequently asked questions

Who is Business Bay for?

People who work in the centre, and investors counting on renting to professionals.

Is the area noisy?

It is a busy business district; homes on the canal promenade are usually quieter than those by the main roads.
