Dubai Hills Estate: off-plan property and the area

Dubai Hills Estate is a large residential community with a park, a golf course and its own mall, between Downtown and Dubai Marina.

In short
  • Apartments, townhouses and villas.
  • A large park and an 18-hole golf course.
  • Dubai Hills Mall.
  • Geared to families.

What the area is like

It is a master-planned community with green spaces, schools and clinics, conveniently placed for both the city centre and the coast.

Who it suits

Families who want greenery and amenities close to home, and people buying a home to live in for the long term.

Transport

The area sits by Al Khail Road and Umm Suqeim Street; Downtown Dubai and Dubai Marina are an easy drive away.

What homes are sold here

Apartments in complexes by the park and the mall, townhouses and villas, including by the golf course. The community is a joint venture of Emaar and Meraas.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a square metre cost in Dubai Hills Estate?

According to the Dubai Land Department (DLD), the median sale price in Dubai Hills Estate over the last 12 months is AED 25,724 per m² (data as of 2026-10-08). In 2023 the median was AED 22,501 per m². That is about AED 2,390 per square foot.

How much is an apartment in Dubai Hills Estate?

Median DLD transaction prices over 12 months in Dubai Hills Estate: studio — AED 970,000, 1 bedroom — AED 1,740,000, 2 bedrooms — AED 2,680,000. This is the median across all deals in the area, not a valuation of a specific unit.

What share of deals in Dubai Hills Estate are off-plan?

53% of sales in Dubai Hills Estate over the last 12 months were off-plan purchases, according to DLD data.

Who is the developer of Dubai Hills Estate?

The community is a joint venture of Emaar and Meraas.

Is the area good for families?

Yes: there is plenty of greenery, schools and a large park.
