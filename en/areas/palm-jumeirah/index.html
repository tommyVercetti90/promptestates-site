Palm Jumeirah: off-plan property and the area

Palm Jumeirah is a palm-shaped man-made island and one of Dubai’s most recognisable addresses, with beachfront residences, villas and hotels.

In short
  • Beachfront apartments and villas.
  • The premium end of the market.
  • Resort hotels and restaurants.
  • Many developments have their own beaches.

What the area is like

The island is made up of a trunk lined with residential buildings and fronds lined with villas. It is home to well-known hotels and beach clubs.

Who it suits

Buyers of premium homes by the sea, and investors counting on rentals at the top end of the market.

Transport

A monorail runs along the island’s trunk; the island connects to the mainland near Sheikh Zayed Road, and to the outer crescent by an undersea tunnel.

What homes are sold here

Apartments in residences on the trunk and crescent, villas on the fronds. New projects are most often branded beachfront residences.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a square metre cost in Palm Jumeirah?

According to the Dubai Land Department (DLD), the median sale price in Palm Jumeirah over the last 12 months is AED 38,301 per m² (data as of 2026-10-08). In 2023 the median was AED 29,529 per m². That is about AED 3,558 per square foot.

How much is an apartment in Palm Jumeirah?

Median DLD transaction prices over 12 months in Palm Jumeirah: studio — AED 1,480,000, 1 bedroom — AED 3,380,000, 2 bedrooms — AED 6,020,000. This is the median across all deals in the area, not a valuation of a specific unit.

What share of deals in Palm Jumeirah are off-plan?

31% of sales in Palm Jumeirah over the last 12 months were off-plan purchases, according to DLD data.

Who is the developer of the island?

Palm Jumeirah was created by Nakheel.

Is the island good for renting out?

Demand is strong in the premium segment, including holiday lets; the entry price is high too.
