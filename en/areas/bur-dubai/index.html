Bur Dubai: new apartment projects and the area

Bur Dubai is the historic heart of the city on the western bank of Dubai Creek, opposite Deira. Most buildings here are older, but new off-plan towers are rising in Wasl 1 next to Zabeel Park and in Dubai Healthcare City.

In short
  • Old Dubai: Al Fahidi Historical Neighbourhood, the souks and abra boats across the Creek.
  • Two metro lines: the Red and Green lines meet at BurJuman.
  • New projects are concentrated in Wasl 1 (Al Kifaf) and Dubai Healthcare City (Umm Hurair).
  • Few new launches compared with JVC or Business Bay — supply is limited.

What the area is like

Bur Dubai covers Al Souq Al Kabeer, Al Fahidi, Mankhool, Al Raffa, Al Karama, Oud Metha, Umm Hurair and Al Kifaf. It is dense and lived-in: older mid-rise apartment blocks, shops, schools, hospitals and the BurJuman mall, with Downtown and DIFC 10–15 minutes away by car.

Who it suits

People who want a central address with the metro on the doorstep, and investors looking for rental demand from the many people who work nearby. The choice of new buildings is narrow, so compare each project carefully.

Where the new projects are

Wasl 1 in Al Kifaf, between Zabeel Park and Sheikh Zayed Road, is a large master project with Park Views Residences and Avenue Park Towers. In Dubai Healthcare City (Umm Hurair) smaller residential buildings are under construction, including Azizi Emerald.

Transport

BurJuman is the interchange of the Red and Green metro lines; Al Fahidi, Al Ghubaiba, Oud Metha, ADCB and Al Jafiliya stations also serve the area. Abras and the water bus cross the Creek to Deira.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a square metre cost in Bur Dubai?

According to the Dubai Land Department (DLD), the median sale price in Bur Dubai over the last 12 months is AED 23,827 per m² (data as of 2026-10-08). In 2023 the median was AED 20,117 per m². That is about AED 2,214 per square foot.

How much is an apartment in Bur Dubai?

Median DLD transaction prices over 12 months in Bur Dubai: 1 bedroom — AED 1,870,000, 2 bedrooms — AED 2,690,000. This is the median across all deals in the area, not a valuation of a specific unit.

What share of deals in Bur Dubai are off-plan?

59% of sales in Bur Dubai over the last 12 months were off-plan purchases, according to DLD data.

Are there new apartment projects in Bur Dubai?

Yes, but only a few: according to the DLD project registry, six residential projects are under construction in Bur Dubai, mostly in Wasl 1 near Zabeel Park and in Dubai Healthcare City.

Bur Dubai or Deira?

They face each other across Dubai Creek. Bur Dubai has the Wasl 1 master plan and two metro lines; Deira is denser and more commercial, and its new waterfront supply is on Dubai Islands.
