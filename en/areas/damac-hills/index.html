DAMAC Hills: off-plan property and the area

DAMAC Hills is a gated community by developer DAMAC around the Trump International Golf Club Dubai course. It has villas, townhouses and apartments overlooking the golf course and parks.

In short
  • A community by developer DAMAC.
  • Trump International Golf Club Dubai at its centre.
  • Villas, townhouses and apartments.
  • Parks, schools and sports facilities within the community.

What the area is like

DAMAC Hills sits off Al Qudra Road and Hessa Street, near Arjan and Motor City. Inside the community are the golf course, parks, schools and shops.

Who it suits

Families who want a villa or townhouse in a green gated community, and those who value golf and sport close to home. Do not confuse it with the separate DAMAC Hills 2 community.

Transport

Access to Al Qudra Road, Hessa Street and Sheikh Mohammed Bin Zayed Road; Dubai Marina and Downtown are about a 20–25-minute drive. There is no metro.

What homes are sold here

Villas and townhouses, plus apartments in complexes overlooking the golf course. DAMAC keeps launching new phases.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a square metre cost in DAMAC Hills?

According to the Dubai Land Department (DLD), the median sale price in DAMAC Hills over the last 12 months is AED 18,523 per m² (data as of 2026-10-08). In 2023 the median was AED 15,809 per m². That is about AED 1,721 per square foot.

How much is an apartment in DAMAC Hills?

Median DLD transaction prices over 12 months in DAMAC Hills: studio — AED 610,000, 1 bedroom — AED 1,240,000, 2 bedrooms — AED 2,030,000. This is the median across all deals in the area, not a valuation of a specific unit.

What share of deals in DAMAC Hills are off-plan?

52% of sales in DAMAC Hills over the last 12 months were off-plan purchases, according to DLD data.

Are DAMAC Hills and DAMAC Hills 2 the same?

No. DAMAC Hills is the community around the golf club off Al Qudra Road; DAMAC Hills 2 is a separate, more remote and more affordable area.

Is it good for families?

Yes: there are schools, parks, sports facilities and shops inside.
