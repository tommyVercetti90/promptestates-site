MBR City and Meydan: off-plan property and the area

Mohammed Bin Rashid Al Maktoum City (MBR City) is a large district between Downtown and Ras Al Khor. It includes Meydan, Sobha Hartland, District One and complexes on the Dubai Canal.

In short
  • A 10–15-minute drive to Downtown.
  • Villas with lagoons, townhouses and apartments.
  • The Meydan racecourse and plenty of green space.
  • Developers include Sobha, Meydan, Azizi, Ellington and others.

What the area is like

MBR City brings together several communities: Sobha Hartland with villas and apartments among greenery, District One with a crystal lagoon, Meydan with its racecourse and Azizi Riviera along the Dubai Canal.

Who it suits

Families who want a villa or a large apartment close to the centre, and investors counting on the area's growth as it is built out.

Transport

The area borders Al Khail Road and Meydan Road; Downtown and Business Bay are a 10–15-minute drive away. Most of the area has no metro.

What homes are sold here

Villas and townhouses by lagoons and parks, as well as apartments in Sobha Hartland, Meydan and along the Dubai Canal.

Frequently asked questions

How does MBR City differ from Dubai Hills?

Both are green and family-oriented, but MBR City is closer to Downtown and includes several different communities with lagoons.

Are there many off-plan projects here?

Yes, the area is still being built out, with many projects under construction from different developers.
