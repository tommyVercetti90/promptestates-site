What Dubai Islands are

Dubai Islands are the northernmost man-made islands on Dubai's coast. They sit at the mouth of Dubai Creek, opposite historic Deira, and were formerly called Deira Islands.

Under Nakheel's master plan the islands are becoming a low-density resort destination: villas and mid-rise apartments, beachfront hotels, golf, marinas and lots of greenery. Island A is partly under construction, and the concepts for islands B–E have been updated to a resort format.

Five islands, five characters

A

Island A — the urban core

Energy, a mall, marinas and waterfront living

area: 510 haPartly under construction
  • The islands' main mall and entertainment
  • Marinas, promenades and beachfront hotels
  • Mid- and high-rise apartments — most of the islands' homes
  • A waterfront art and cultural district
B

Island B — resorts & communities

Beach hotels and calm neighbourhoods

area: 323 ha
  • Beachfront resorts along the western channel
  • Beachfront villas and low-density communities
  • Waterfront hotels and apartments along the eastern channel
  • Parks, sports grounds and community clubs
C

Island C — the oasis

Golf, wellness and family holidays

area: 778 haThe largest island
  • An 18-hole golf course and golf residences
  • Wellness, eco and family resorts
  • A marina village with dining and shops
  • An oasis park linking every district
D

Island D — the sports club

Country club, golf and water

area: 156 ha
  • A 9-hole golf course
  • A sports club: football, tennis academy, pools, archery, horse riding
  • A boutique marina and beach club
  • Low-density residences with club membership
E

Island E — the estates

A private island for the finest villas

area: 99 haThe most private
  • Signature estate lots

Frequently asked questions

Are Dubai Islands and Deira Islands the same?

Yes. The islands off Deira were renamed Dubai Islands; the master developer is Nakheel.

What is already built?

Under the master plan, island A is planned and partly under construction, while islands B–E have been repositioned to a low-density resort format. Check each project's status in PromptEstates cards.

Can I get a Golden Visa?

Yes, if the property is worth AED 2 million or more and the developer is DLD-approved. See the Golden Visa article.

How do Dubai Islands differ from Palm Jumeirah?

The Palm is established and pricier. Dubai Islands are a new area with beaches, golf and marinas, more projects under construction and a lower entry price.
