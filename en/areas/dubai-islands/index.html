What Dubai Islands are

Dubai Islands are the northernmost man-made islands on Dubai's coast. They sit at the mouth of Dubai Creek, opposite historic Deira, and were formerly called Deira Islands.

Under Nakheel's master plan the islands are becoming a low-density resort destination: villas and mid-rise apartments, beachfront hotels, golf, marinas and lots of greenery. Island A is partly under construction, and the concepts for islands B–E have been updated to a resort format.

Where Dubai Islands are and how to get there

Dubai Islands lie in the Arabian Gulf off the Deira coast, at the mouth of Dubai Creek, next to Al Mamzar Beach Park and close to the border with Sharjah. Road bridges link the islands to Deira: Dubai International Airport (DXB) is about 10 minutes away by car, Downtown Dubai and Burj Khalifa about 15–20 minutes.

The project was earlier called Deira Islands, and before that Palm Deira. The Dubai Land Department (DLD) registry still lists the area as Palm Deira, so that is the name to look for in title deeds and DLD data. Island A, the urban core of the master plan, holds most of the apartments and is partly under construction.

Five islands, five characters

Island A — the urban core

Energy, a mall, marinas and waterfront living

area: 510 haPartly under construction
  • The islands' main mall and entertainment
  • Marinas, promenades and beachfront hotels
  • Mid- and high-rise apartments — most of the islands' homes
  • A waterfront art and cultural district

Island B — resorts & communities

Beach hotels and calm neighbourhoods

area: 323 ha
  • Beachfront resorts along the western channel
  • Beachfront villas and low-density communities
  • Waterfront hotels and apartments along the eastern channel
  • Parks, sports grounds and community clubs

Island C — the oasis

Golf, wellness and family holidays

area: 778 haThe largest island
  • An 18-hole golf course and golf residences
  • Wellness, eco and family resorts
  • A marina village with dining and shops
  • An oasis park linking every district

Island D — the sports club

Country club, golf and water

area: 156 ha
  • A 9-hole golf course
  • A sports club: football, tennis academy, pools, archery, horse riding
  • A boutique marina and beach club
  • Low-density residences with club membership

Island E — the estates

A private island for the finest villas

area: 99 haThe most private
  • Signature estate lots
  • A private marina
  • Beachfront and waterfront villas
  • An exclusive clubhouse

Frequently asked questions

How much does a square metre cost in Dubai Islands?

According to the Dubai Land Department (DLD), the median sale price in Dubai Islands over the last 12 months is AED 29,542 per m² (data as of 2026-10-08). In 2023 the median was AED 26,493 per m². That is about AED 2,745 per square foot.

How much is an apartment in Dubai Islands?

Median DLD transaction prices over 12 months in Dubai Islands: studio — AED 1,180,000, 1 bedroom — AED 2,330,000, 2 bedrooms — AED 3,500,000. This is the median across all deals in the area, not a valuation of a specific unit.

What share of deals in Dubai Islands are off-plan?

98% of sales in Dubai Islands over the last 12 months were off-plan purchases, according to DLD data.

How many off-plan projects are on sale on Dubai Islands?

PromptEstates lists 25 off-plan projects on Dubai Islands with launch prices from AED 1.41M and handover between 2026 and 2029. The table on this page shows each project's developer, starting price, handover date and payment plan.

Are Dubai Islands and Deira Islands the same?

Yes. The islands off Deira were renamed Dubai Islands; the master developer is Nakheel.

What is already built?

Under the master plan, island A is planned and partly under construction, while islands B–E have been repositioned to a low-density resort format. Check each project's status in PromptEstates cards.

Can I get a Golden Visa?

Yes, if the property is worth AED 2 million or more and the developer is DLD-approved. See the Golden Visa article.

How do Dubai Islands differ from Palm Jumeirah?

The Palm is established and pricier. Dubai Islands are a new area with beaches, golf and marinas, more projects under construction and a lower entry price.

Where can I find the Dubai Islands master plan (PDF)?

Nakheel publishes the Dubai Islands master plan in its brochures and shows it at its sales centres. This page shows the Stage 2 Vision Plan with its legend and a description of each of the five islands, A to E.

What is on Island C?

Island C, the oasis, is the largest of the five islands at 778 ha. The master plan puts an 18-hole golf course with golf residences, wellness, eco and family resorts, a marina village and an oasis park there.

Where exactly are Dubai Islands?

Off the Deira coast at the mouth of Dubai Creek, next to Al Mamzar Beach Park — about 10 minutes from DXB airport and 15–20 minutes from Downtown Dubai.
