Questions & answers.

What is PromptEstates?

PromptEstates is an AI service for finding off-plan property in the UAE. You describe what you want in plain words, and the AI matches developer projects and shows market analytics for each one: price per m², area trends, rental yield forecast and payment plan.

How many properties does PromptEstates cover?

Off-plan projects from UAE developers, with prices, payment plans and developer information. The catalogue is built on Dubai Land Department open data and grows every week.

Which cities and emirates does it cover?

Dubai, Abu Dhabi and other UAE emirates. You can search by a specific area — such as Dubai Marina, Business Bay or JVC — or simply describe what matters: waterfront, near the metro, for rental.

How does the AI choose projects?

The AI matches your budget, area, handover date and goal — to live in or to rent out — against developer projects, area price per m², handover schedules and rental demand, then explains why each option made the shortlist.

How much does PromptEstates cost?

The Dubai market overview and the area map are free. Pro costs AED 179 a month or AED 1,490 a year (31% less), with the first 14 days free, and unlocks every area, project and transaction, the AI analyst and memos. Cancel in one click.

Is the analytics investment advice?

No. PromptEstates analytics and forecasts are for information only and help you compare projects; they do not constitute investment advice.

What is off-plan property?

Off-plan means buying a home from the developer before construction is finished. Payment usually follows a plan: a share on booking, a share during construction and the rest on handover or after it (post-handover). In Dubai, buyers’ payments for such projects go into the project’s escrow account.

What is the DLD fee when buying property in Dubai?

It is the Dubai Land Department registration fee — 4% of the property price. PromptEstates includes it in the payment plan total, so you compare projects on the full cost rather than the advertised price.

Can buying property in the UAE get me a Golden Visa?

Yes: property worth AED 2M+ by DLD valuation qualifies for the Golden Visa — including off-plan from an approved developer or a mortgaged home with a bank NOC. On a smaller budget there is the 2-year investor visa: since April 2026 a sole owner of a completed Dubai home faces no minimum value. PromptEstates project cards carry a Gold visa mark.

Which languages does PromptEstates support?

The app interface is available in 10 languages: English, Russian, Arabic, Chinese, French, Turkish, Hindi, Italian, German and Spanish. This website is available in 11 languages.

What is the difference between Free and Pro?

Free gives you the Dubai market overview and the interactive area map. Pro unlocks every area and project, 24 months of transactions, developer reliability, property-type filters, the AI analyst, AI memos, fair price and exports.

Can I cancel?

Yes, anytime from your Stripe portal — no emails or calls. Access stays until the end of the paid period and auto-renewal stops immediately.

Does the subscription work in the app too?

Yes. PromptEstates has one account for the dashboard and the app: sign in with Google, Apple or email and the subscription works everywhere.
