Dubai property market reports

Every quarter we break down the Dubai market from registered Dubai Land Department (DLD) transactions: how much sold and at what price, what is happening with off-plan, mortgages and rents, and where demand is moving. All reports are collected here, newest first.

In short
  • Official Dubai Land Department data and Ejari rent contracts only, no advertised prices.
  • Each report covers sales and value, median per m² and sq ft, off-plan vs ready, mortgages, rents and the busiest areas.
  • Compared with the same quarter last year and with the previous quarter.
  • Tables as CSV and infographics as PNG, free for publications with a credit to the source.

What is in each report

The quarter's key numbers in one table, a chart of sales by month, off-plan and ready prices, mortgages and rents, a table of the 15 busiest areas and what it means for buyers. Area figures link to the area pages with 12 months of detailed data.

Where the data comes from

Sales come from the Dubai Land Department Real Estate Transactions open dataset on data.dubai, rents from Ejari contracts. The quarterly median is the monthly medians of price per m², weighted by the number of sales. We check the totals against figures published by market analysts.

Using the data in publications

Numbers, tables and charts may be used in articles, research and social media with a credit: “PromptEstates, based on Dubai Land Department data”. Every chart comes with ready-made embed code. Press enquiries: first@promptestates.com.
