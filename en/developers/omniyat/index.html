Omniyat: projects and off-plan homes

Omniyat is a private Dubai developer founded in 2005. It specialises in architecturally ambitious ultra-premium projects.

In short
  • Founded in 2005.
  • The Opus in Business Bay was designed by Zaha Hadid Architects.
  • On Palm Jumeirah: One at Palm Jumeirah, ORLA, AVA.
  • Works with hospitality brands, including Dorchester Collection.

What it builds

Omniyat builds one-of-a-kind residences and penthouses with signature architecture and hotel-style service — on Palm Jumeirah and in Business Bay. It is a segment of high ticket sizes and few units.

What to check before buying

Compare the project’s price with the area average, check the payment plan and handover date, and confirm the project’s RERA registration and escrow account. Even with a major developer, terms vary a lot from project to project.
